Dashboard

Account Setup & Overview
Step 1: Set Up Account
✓ Set up your account by completing initial registration and verifying your access details.
Step 2: Navigate Dashboard
✓ Navigate to your marketplace dashboard to access featured products and services.
2

Services

Service Exploration & Catalog Filtering
Step 1: Browse Services
✓ Browse the different services on the dashboard to review available items and solutions.
Step 2: Filter Price Range
✓ You can choose the price range of products for you to browse.
3

Orders

Selection, Cart Management & Checkout
Step 1: Choose Category
✓ Click on a category you are interested in purchasing from.
Step 2: Add to Bag
✓ Click on the "Add to Bag" button to add the item to your cart.
Step 3: Adjust Quantity
✓ Adjust the quantity to your preference and click update cart.
Step 4: Proceed to Checkout
✓ Proceed to the checkout by clicking on the proceed button.
Step 5: Shipping Information
✓ Fill in the shipping information, making sure it is accurate to avoid any future issues with delivery.
4

Payments

Payment Gateway & Order Tracking
Step 1: Choose Payment Method
✓ Choose your preferred payment method.
Step 2: Place Order
✓ Click on place order to proceed to the payment page.
Step 3: Mobile Money Prompt
✓ Wait for a mobile money prompt on your phone number and input the necessary information.
Step 4: Track Order Status
✓ You can track your order from pending to delivered in real time.

Profile

Profile Management & Order Autofill
Step 1: Edit Profile
✓ You can edit your profile which is autofilled into your shipping information when placing an order.
