📂 Contacts & Tools

Contact Groups

If you intend to send bulk sms to the same group of people multiple times, create a contact group that can be reused again instead of always uploading a file or pasting numbers.


✓ Step 1: Click on the New Group button.
✓ Step 2: Fill in the details: Group Name, Upload .txt File (one number per line), Or Paste Numbers.

Templates

If you have a particular message you want to send repeatedly, create a template to save time and ensure consistency.


✓ Step 1: Click on the create template button.
✓ Step 2: Fill in the details: Template Name *, Channel (Choose channel for which you want your contact groups to apply), and Message Body.
💬 Bulk SMS

Send Bulk SMS

✓ Step 1: Choose your Recipients (Select a Contact Group from created ones, Upload .txt File, or Paste Numbers - one per line or comma-separated).
✓ Step 2: Fill in your preferred Sender ID.
✓ Step 3: Compose your message and click send.

Schedule SMS

✓ Step 1: Select your Contact Group Or Upload .txt Or Paste Numbers.
✓ Step 2: Choose the Delivery Date & Time and fill in the Sender ID.
✓ Step 4: Choose your Message template (the one you created). You can edit it to your liking and send.

Custom SMS

✓ Step 1: Fill in your Campaign Name & Sender ID.
✓ Step 2: Upload the Spreadsheet with the names, respective numbers and custom messages to be sent to each number (.csv, .xlsx, or .xls). You can also download a sample template for guidance
✓ Step 3: Preview the imported file and verify all details.
✓ Step 4: Compose the message by clicking on the fields in the spreadsheet.
✓ Step 5: If the message under the preview box is to your liking proceed to review.
✓ Step 6: Verify your message template, confirm and send.

Custom SMS History

✓ Review previous custom campaign dispatches, status logs, and download historical reports via messaging history.
✉️ Bulk Emails

Send Email

✓ Step 1: To save time pasting the same multiple recipient emails over and over, create an email list. Scroll to email lists, click on create list and fill in list name & description.
✓ Step 2: After your email list has been created, click on Manage and paste the recipient emails under subscribers.
✓ Step 3: Navigate to Compose your email and click on "from list" to choose your created list of recipients.
✓ Step 4: Fill in the name and email that the recipient will see.
✓ Step 5: Fill in your email subject line & body and send email campaign.
✓ Step 6: Under email settings, you can set a default name and email that recipients will always see.

Schedule Email

✓ Select target email list, specify future delivery date/time, and finalize scheduled broadcast.

Email History

✓ Track email delivery performance, view sent message logs, and inspect bounce records.
🟢 WhatsApp Campaigns

Send Bulk WhatsApp

✓ Step 1: Choose your Recipients (Select a Contact Group, Upload .txt File, or Paste Numbers - one per line or comma-separated).
✓ Step 2: Compose your text message and click send whatsapp messages.

Schedule WhatsApp

✓ Step 1: Choose your recipients and pick your target delivery date and time for WhatsApp dispatch.
✓ Step 2: Compose your WhatsApp message body and click schedule.
